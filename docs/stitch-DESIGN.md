## Brand & Style

This design system serves high-end editorial wellness and cellular nutrition targeted at discerning, design-conscious women aged 35–40. The aesthetic sits at the precise nexus of progressive digital luxury and organic tactile intimacy—where the fluid 3D physics and computational elegance of digital art houses converge with the quiet, sensorial restraint of high-end editorial apothecaries.

The visual direction rejects both cold clinical medicalism and stereotypical pastel wellness tropes. Instead, it creates an immersive, contemplative atmosphere driven by warm mineral tones, kinetic editorial typography, and layered translucent substrates. The emotional tone is authoritative yet deeply restorative, scientific yet sensual, and unmistakably bespoke.

Key stylistic tenets include:

- **Tactile Vellum & Frosted Substrates:** Multi-tiered depth created through translucent surface layering rather than harsh spatial elevation.
- **Editorial Kinetic Balance:** Monumental, razor-sharp serif headlines contrasted against micro-detailed, functional geometric grotesque labeling.
- **Organic Fluidity:** Deliberately generous spatial cadence, soft pill silhouettes, and floating contextual badges mimicking biological cellular membranes.

## Colors

The palette is derived from mineral clays, dried botanicals, and raw cellular nutrients, grounded in warmth and tactile realism.

- **Surface Philosophy:** The canvas relies on `bone-cream` (#FBFAF6) and `warm-ivory` (#F8F6F1) as pure, non-sterile light baselines. Surfaces transition through subtle soft-graded fields into `warm-sand` (#EFE7D9) for layered cards and embedded sensory trays.
- **Primary Mineral Tone:** `deep-clay` (#D69A7C) serves as the primary chromatic driver, conveying regenerative earth, circulation, and vitality. Its lighter derivative `terracotta-clay` (#E7B7A0) acts as an illuminating focal point for interactive highlights.
- **Secondary Botanical Tone:** `olive` (#8E9480) and `deep-sage` (#AEB89E) provide grounding cellular balance, symbolizing herbal formulation and quiet homeostasis. `sage` (#CFD7C4) provides subtle tint washes across translucent containers.
- **Accent Resonance:** `deep-rose` (#C98C86) and `muted-rose` (#D8ABA6) offer visceral warmth, used sparingly for physiological markers, badges, and focal interactive elements.
- **Text & Anchors:** `graphite-charcoal` (#23221E) replaces absolute pitch black. It provides deep, high-contrast legibility while preserving the organic warmth of the surrounding cream environment. Pure white (#FFFFFF) is reserved strictly for high-specular glass reflex highlights.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy executes an intentional tension between classical high-fashion editorial display and ergonomic geometric precision.

- **Display & Headline Hierarchy:** Bodoni Moda embodies dramatic high-contrast letterforms reminiscent of avant-garde apothecary publications and luxury print mastheads. In headlines, italics should be applied selectively to individual evocative words (e.g., _cellular_, _synergy_, _infusion_) to introduce kinetic cadence without impairing scannability.
- **Body & Structural Text:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides clean, humanistic geometry with generous counters and optical balance. Rendered in weights 300 (Light) and 400 (Regular), it prevents reading fatigue and ensures that scientific formulations, clinical data, and regimen guides remain crisp and accessible.
- **Meta & Labeling Track:** Category identifiers, scientific dosage figures, and protocol labels use `label-caps` in uppercase tracking (+0.14em) to deliver a laboratory-grade, curated clinical aesthetic.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architected around an asymmetrical fluid grid, drawing from the compositional pacing of art monographs and digital architectural studies.

- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column desktop grid transitions to an 8-column tablet grid and a 4-column mobile grid. Large editorial statements break standard boundaries via column offsets (e.g., headlines spanning columns 2 through 10, offset from body text spanning columns 5 through 11).
- **Rhythm & Negative Space:** The design system prioritizes extensive negative space (`space-xl` and canvas margins up to 4rem) to communicate exclusivity, calm, and biological unhurriedness. Content does not compete for density; it floats within clearly demarcated atmospheric regions.
- **Layer Stacking:** Content modules employ horizontal displacement and staggered vertical overlaps (e.g., an elevated vellum card protruding into an adjacent image container by 2rem) to realize the "Layer Method" physically across the Z-axis.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation is achieved through tactile transparency, optical light refraction, and soft tonal diffusion rather than structural dropshadows.

- **Vellum Layering:** Overlapping content containers employ backdrop filtration (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(140%)`) coupled with semi-translucent fills (`rgba(251, 250, 246, 0.72)` or `rgba(239, 231, 217, 0.65)`). This generates physical separation while allowing colors and imagery underneath to softly bleed through.
- **Ambient Chromatic Glows:** When floating elements require separation from the background, shadows are tinted using the warm mineral spectrum rather than desaturated black. Shadow values utilize expansive diffusion: `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(214, 154, 124, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -6px rgba(35, 34, 30, 0.04)`.
- **Specular Edge Highlighting:** Containers possess razor-thin top and inner hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`) to simulate the bevel of hand-cut laboratory glassware catching light.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes softened structural forms that reflect cellular capsules, smoothed river stones, and biological membranes.

- **Corner Radii Hierarchy:** Standard interactive components and structured cards utilize `roundedness: 2` (base radius of 0.5rem / 8px). Larger editorial cards and translucent trays scale to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) and `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) to preserve visual softness across wide surface areas.
- **Cellular Badges & Floating Pills:** Pill-shaped contours (`rounded-full` / 9999px) are dedicated exclusively to interactive status chips, dosage meters, category markers, and primary kinetic call-to-actions.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary Interactive:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), clad in `deep-clay` (#D69A7C) with sharp `bone-cream` (#FBFAF6) typography. On hover, smooth transition into `graphite-charcoal` (#23221E) accompanied by an interior 1px specular halo. Padding: 0.875rem 2rem.
- **Secondary Ghost:** Pill-shaped with a 1px border rendered in `graphite-charcoal` at 20% opacity. Text in `graphite-charcoal`. On hover, the container fills with `warm-sand` (#EFE7D9) at 50% opacity and border opacity shifts to 100%.
- **Text Action:** `label-caps` typography paired with an animated hairline underline that expands outward from center, tinted in `deep-rose` (#C98C86).

### Cards & Vellum Containers

- **Sensory Layer Cards:** Crafted with multi-layered backgrounds. Surface is set to `bone-cream` with 80% opacity, a 16px backdrop blur, 1.5rem corner radius, and an internal padding of `space-lg`. Enclosed by an ultra-thin 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.
- **Product & Formulation Tiles:** Set upon `warm-sand` (#EFE7D9) at 40% opacity. Image placements within tiles feature subtle organic masking and generous negative space around product bottles or nutrient textures.

### Chips & Protocol Badges

- **Status & Ingredient Markers:** Ultra-compact pill silhouettes featuring 0.375rem vertical and 0.875rem horizontal padding. Typography set in `label-caps`.
- **Palette Variants:** Sage badges use `sage` (#CFD7C4) at 35% opacity with `olive` (#8E9480) text; clay badges use `terracotta-clay` (#E7B7A0) at 30% opacity with `deep-clay` (#D69A7C) text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors

- **Text Fields:** Minimalist structural lines. Base state features a transparent background with a 1px solid bottom boundary in `graphite-charcoal` (20% opacity). Active focus state transitions the border to `deep-clay` (#D69A7C) with an upward floating label in `label-caps`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Circular geometric toggles (20px diameter) referencing cellular rings. Inactive state is a 1.5px ring in `graphite-charcoal` (25% opacity). Checked state fills with `deep-clay` (#D69A7C) holding a centered, miniature solid ivory sphere (6px).

### Lists & Protocol Sequences

- **Editorial Data Rows:** Separated by horizontal hairlines rendered in `graphite-charcoal` at 10% opacity. Each list item is allocated `space-md` vertical padding, with step indices displayed in uppercase `label-caps` in `deep-rose` (#C98C86) positioned adjacent to primary Bodoni Moda titles.

### Kinetic Vellum Trays (Specialized)

- Floating contextual toolbars and formulation summary drawers fixed to the bottom viewport. Built using frosted vellum styling with an integrated diffuse ambient shadow, housing dosage progress meters and dynamic ingredient breakdowns.
